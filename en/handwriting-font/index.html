Your handwriting as a font? For GoodNotes there’s a better way

Updated November 2, 2026

In short

For Word, Pages or Canva, a font made from your handwriting is the right choice, for example with Calligraphr. In GoodNotes, though, a font stays typed text: every e looks the same and there are no strokes to erase. Siebwrite turns your text into real strokes in your handwriting instead, which you can erase in GoodNotes.

When a font is the better choice

When you need your handwriting on a computer: in Word or Pages, in Canva, on invitations or for a cutting plotter. For that you need a real font file, and Siebwrite doesn’t make one. A well-known route is Calligraphr: fill in a template, upload it, download your font. The free tier covers up to 75 characters with two variants each, and you need an account.

When Siebwrite is the better choice

When the text should end up in GoodNotes: study notes, flashcards, lecture notes, a letter on your iPad. Then you don’t want a font, you want handwriting that behaves like your own. You erase a word, move a line with the lasso and keep writing next to it with your pen.

Siebwrite uses your real strokes for that. You write each character once, 80 of them, and every time a letter appears it’s drawn a little differently. So a paragraph doesn’t look stamped.

What about a keyboard in my handwriting?

Lots of people look for a keyboard that types straight in their own handwriting. But a keyboard can only enter text, and in GoodNotes text always shows up in a font. The way that works is the other way round: type in Siebwrite and bring the text into GoodNotes as handwriting. Step-by-step guide

Frequently asked questions

Does Siebwrite give me a font file (.ttf)?

No. Siebwrite makes finished pages as a .goodnotes file and a PDF, not a font to install. For Word or Canva, use a font generator.

Will every word look the same?

No. Siebwrite changes each letter a little every time it appears, in size, slant and spacing. If you write several variants of a character, they take turns too.

How long does capturing my handwriting take?

With Siebwrite about five minutes: 80 characters, each written once, with a stylus, finger or mouse. You can add variants later, but you don’t have to.

Does it cost anything?

Siebwrite is free, with no account, no watermark and no limit on how many pages you export.
