Questions

Can GoodNotes turn typed text into handwriting by itself?

No. GoodNotes converts handwriting to text and tidies messy writing with Restyle, but it doesn't turn typed text into your handwriting (as of November 2026). That's exactly what Siebwrite does. Step-by-step guide

Does it cost anything?

No. No subscription, no ads, no watermark and no limit on how many pages you export.

Can I really erase it in GoodNotes?

Yes, really. The file contains ballpoint strokes, not pictures, so the eraser and the lasso work the same as on anything you write yourself.

Is it a font?

No. A font looks the same every time a letter repeats and ends up in GoodNotes as a text box. Siebwrite draws each letter a little differently and gives you real strokes you can erase and move with the lasso. Font or strokes: the difference

Can I paste text I copied?

Yes. Anything you can copy works: from Word, a website, your notes or a chatbot. Long texts are spread across several pages automatically.

How long does the alphabet take?

About five minutes with a pen, for 80 characters. You can add variants later so it looks more natural, but you don't have to.

Is it only for German?

No. The English version leaves out the German umlauts (ä, ö, ü, ß), though you can still add them later. The ready-made handwritings also have accented letters like é, ñ and ç.

Are there ready-made handwritings?

Five: Cursive, Elegant, Quill, Print and Playful. They're built from free single-line fonts, and each character comes in a few slightly different shapes. In GoodNotes they erase like your own handwriting. Try one now

Can I use any color?

Any color. Next to the four fixed ones, the + in the app opens a color picker. Your pick shows up in the PDF and in GoodNotes too.

Do I need an iPad?

Not at all. Writing works on any device, and a pen makes it nicer. You open the .goodnotes file in GoodNotes. Don't have GoodNotes? Use the PDF.

Where is my handwriting stored?

In your browser. If you sign in with Google, also in your own Google Drive. More under Privacy.
