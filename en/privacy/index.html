Privacy

As of 2 November 2026

Siebwrite has no server of its own, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your handwriting stays in your browser unless you choose to sign in with Google.

This is a translation for your convenience. The German version is the legally binding one.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the time and the requested file, for example in server logs. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a reachable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More in GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts

Your browser loads the Newsreader and IBM Plex fonts from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Google receives your IP address in the process. More in Google's privacy policy.

Storage in your browser

The site keeps your handwriting, text and settings in your browser's storage (localStorage) so they are still there on your next visit. This is necessary for the site to work (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Siebwrite sets no cookies. You can delete everything with "Delete all" in the app or by clearing the site data in your browser.

Transfer and Save

"Transfer" creates a link containing your handwriting, text and settings. They sit behind the # sign, a part the browser never sends to the server. "Save" stores the same as a file on your device. Where you pass on the link or the file is up to you.

Sign in with Google

Optional. Only if you sign in does the following happen:

  • Your browser loads a script from Google (accounts.google.com) and you sign in with Google. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Siebwrite only gets access to its own hidden folder in your Google Drive (the "drive.appdata" permission), not to your other files. Your handwriting is stored there as a file so it reaches your other devices.
  • Siebwrite reads the name, email address and profile picture of your Google account to show them in the top right corner and to suggest the account the next time you sign in. Your browser loads the profile picture directly from Google. This information and an access key that is valid for about an hour stay in your browser.
  • Siebwrite has no server of its own. Nobody but you and Google gets your data; it is not sold, not passed on and not used for advertising.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time: in the app via your profile picture in the top right corner → "Sign out", or in your Google account under Third-party apps with account access. To delete the file in the hidden folder, go to Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Siebwrite → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Siebwrite's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), and you can withdraw consent at any time. Because Siebwrite itself stores nothing about you, your data is in your browser and, if you signed in, in your Google account. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one in Lower Saxony (lfd.niedersachsen.de).
